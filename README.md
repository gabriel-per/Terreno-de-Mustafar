# Terreno-de-Mustafar
Projeto de geração procedural e exibição de terreno para a disciplina de Introdução às Técnicas de Programação do curso de Ciência da Computação na UFRN.

---
## Real backstory
Last week, Vader called to tell us he decided to decorate the empty halls of his fortress on Mustafar with photographs of the planet's terrain. However, the cloudy atmosphere of the planet won't allow us to take clear images of the ground. Of course, we don't want to mess with Lord Vader and let him down, so we decided to use artificially generated images instead. I don't think he'll notice the difference whatsoever.
